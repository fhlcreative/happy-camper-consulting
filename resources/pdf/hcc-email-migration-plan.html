Email & Domain Migration Plan

Happy Camper Consulting • Prepared March 2, 2026

What's happening: Your website is hosted on Vercel, but your email and domain are still on Chillidog Hosting. Because these are split across two systems, email routing has been unreliable — including MachForms notifications.

The fix: Move email to Google Workspace and eventually transfer the domain to Namecheap. This gives you reliable email, a clean DNS setup, and removes Chillidog from the equation.

New Setup & Costs

Total new cost: approximately $7/month + $13/year for domain renewal.

Migration Steps

1Sign Up for Google Workspace

  1. Go to workspace.google.com
  2. Choose the Business Starter plan ($7/month)
  3. Enter your domain: happycamperconsultant.com
  4. Create your admin account: jeff@happycamperconsultant.com
  5. Google will ask you to verify domain ownership (see Step 2)

2Update DNS Records in Chillidog

Log into Chillidog cPanel → Zone Editor and make these changes:

a) Add Google's verification TXT record

Google will give you a TXT record during signup. Add it:

Type: TXT
Name: @
Value: (the verification string Google provides)

b) Delete the old MX record

Remove the existing MX record pointing to bell.chillidoghosting.com

c) Add Google's MX records

Add all 5 of these:

Priority 1 → ASPMX.L.GOOGLE.COM
Priority 5 → ALT1.ASPMX.L.GOOGLE.COM
Priority 5 → ALT2.ASPMX.L.GOOGLE.COM
Priority 10 → ALT3.ASPMX.L.GOOGLE.COM
Priority 10 → ALT4.ASPMX.L.GOOGLE.COM

d) Update the SPF record

Edit the existing TXT record that starts with v=spf1 and replace it with:

v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com +ip4:35.212.113.249 ~all

This authorizes both Google and SiteGround (MachForms) to send email for your domain.

Note: Keep the existing A records (@ pointing to Vercel, forms pointing to SiteGround) and the www CNAME — do not change those.

3Migrate Your Old Emails

Pull your existing emails from Chillidog into Google Workspace:

  1. Log into admin.google.com
  2. Go to Account → Data migration
  3. Choose Email as the migration type
  4. Select Other IMAP server and enter:
IMAP Server: chillimail.chillidoghosting.com
Port: 993
Encryption: SSL
Username: jeff@happycamperconsultant.com
Password: (your current Chillidog email password)

Google will pull all your old emails into Gmail. This runs in the background and may take a few hours.

4Update MachForms Email Settings

Log into MachForms admin → Settings → System Settings:

  1. Check "Use SMTP Server to Send Emails"
  2. Enter these settings:
SMTP Server: smtp.gmail.com
Port: 587
Use TLS/SSL: Yes
Use Authentication: Yes
Username: jeff@happycamperconsultant.com
Password: (App Password — see below)

Generating an App Password

  1. Go to myaccount.google.com
  2. Navigate to Security → 2-Step Verification (enable if not already on)
  3. Scroll down to App Passwords
  4. Create a new app password labeled "MachForms"
  5. Google will give you a 16-character code — use that as the SMTP password

5Transfer Domain to Namecheap (After 60 Days)

Your domain was registered less than 60 days ago, so it can't be transferred yet. Once eligible:

  1. Unlock the domain at Chillidog and get the EPP/authorization code
  2. Go to namecheap.com and initiate a domain transfer (~$13)
  3. Enter the authorization code when prompted
  4. Once transferred, set up DNS records at Namecheap:
A       @     → 216.198.79.1 (Vercel)
CNAME   www   → f7254ef3a428f216.vercel-dns-017.com
A       forms → 35.212.113.249 (SiteGround)
MX      (Google Workspace records from Step 2)
TXT     (SPF record from Step 2)

After this, cancel Chillidog Hosting. It will no longer be needed.
